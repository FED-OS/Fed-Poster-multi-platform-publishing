Thanks for using Fed-Poster! This guide helps you find the right support channel quickly. Please choose the option that best matches your situation so the community can help you efficiently.

---

## 🧭 Choose the Right Channel

| If you...                                              | Then...                                          |
|--------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Found a **bug** 🐛                                      | Open a [Bug Report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) |
| Have a **feature idea** ✨                              | Open a [Feature Request](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_request.md) |
| Have a **question** / want to discuss 💭                | Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/FED-OS/Fed-Poster/discussions) |
| Found a **security issue** 🔒                           | See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — **do NOT open a public issue** |
| Want to **contribute code** 🛠️                         | Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)          |
| Need **commercial / agency support** 🏢                 | Email **business@fedpromptly.com**               |

## 💬 GitHub Discussions (Best for Questions)

[Discussions](https://github.com/FED-OS/Fed-Poster/discussions) is the primary place for:

- ❓ How-to questions and usage help
- 💡 Ideas and brainstorming
- 🗣️ Announcements and community chat
- 📣 Show & tell (share what you built with Fed-Poster!)

### Discussion Categories

| Category        | Use for                                  |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| 🙋 Q&A           | Questions with a definitive answer       |
| 💡 Ideas          | Proposals and brainstorming              |
| 📣 Announcements | Project news from maintainers            |
| 🙌 Show and tell | Showcase your projects / integrations    |
| ☕ General        | Anything else community-related          |

> 💡 **Tip:** Search existing discussions before posting — your question may already be answered.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports

1. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/FED-OS/Fed-Poster/issues?q=is%3Aissue) to avoid duplicates.
2. Open a new issue using the **Bug Report** template.
3. Fill in **all** sections — especially steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual behavior, and environment details.
4. Attach screenshots or screen recordings when possible.

Incomplete bug reports may be closed with a `needs info` label until details are provided.

---

## ✨ Feature Requests

1. Check the [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) — it may already be planned.
2. Search existing issues & discussions for similar ideas.
3. Open a **Feature Request** using the template.
4. Explain the *use case* and *problem* you're solving, not just the solution.

---

## 🔒 Security Issues

Security vulnerabilities must **never** be reported via public issues or discussions. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting process (GitHub Security Advisories or `security@fed-poster.example.com`).

---

## 🏢 Commercial & Enterprise Support

For white-label, agency, self-hosting assistance, SLA-backed support, or custom integrations:

- 📧 Email **business@fedpromptly.com**
- 💰 See [PRICING.md](PRICING.md) for plans

## ⏱️ Response Times

| Channel                  | Expected response                 |
|--------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| GitHub Discussions (Q&A) | Community-driven, best-effort (~1–3 days) |
| Bug reports              | Maintainer triage within ~3 business days |
| Feature requests         | Discussed during roadmap planning |
| Security reports         | Acknowledged within 48 hours (see SECURITY.md) |
| Commercial email         | Within 1–2 business days          |

Fed-Poster is a community-driven open-source project. Maintainers are volunteers (unless on a paid plan), so please be patient and kind.

## 📜 Before You Ask: A Quick Checklist

- [ ] I searched Discussions, Issues, and the Wiki.
- [ ] I read the relevant section of [usage.md](usage.md).
- [ ] I'm using a **supported version** (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
- [ ] I can describe what I expected vs. what happened.
- [ ] I can share my environment (browser, OS, Fed-Poster version, self-hosted vs. cloud).
