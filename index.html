Features

Everything you need to post smarter

A complete toolkit for cross-platform posting — built for creators, developers, and communities who value their privacy.

Privacy-First Architecture

Your API tokens and credentials are stored only in your browser's localStorage. No server ever sees them. No account to create, no data harvested, no tracking — ever.

Multi-Platform Composer

Write your message once and broadcast to all 7 connected platforms simultaneously. Toggle individual platforms on or off per post. Images and video supported.

12 Unique Themes

From Deep Space noir to Cyberpunk neon to Synthwave retro — pick your aesthetic. Your theme choice persists across every page automatically.

Content Calendar

Plan your content month by month. Visualize scheduled posts, drag to reschedule, and see your publishing pipeline at a glance.

Analytics Dashboard

Track posts sent, success rates, and per-platform engagement. Visual charts break down your cross-platform performance over time.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Power-user speed: press ? for shortcuts, H for history, T to cycle themes, and number keys to jump between tabs.

How It Works

Three steps to cross-posting

No setup wizard, no email verification, no waiting. Just open and post.

1

Open the Dashboard

Navigate to the dashboard. No login needed — it runs entirely in your browser. Pick your theme and you're ready.

2

Enter Your Tokens

Paste your platform API tokens into each tab. They're saved locally and auto-filled next time. Toggle which platforms to post to.

3

Write & Post All

Compose your message, attach an image, and hit Post All. Fed-Poster sends directly to each platform's API in parallel.
