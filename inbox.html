Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fed-Poster really free? What's the catch?

There's no catch. Fed-Poster is open-source under the MIT license and runs 100% in your browser. There are no servers to maintain, no databases to pay for, and no telemetry to monetize. The Community tier will always be free. Pro is an optional convenience layer for users who want cloud sync and advanced scheduling.

Where are my credentials and post data stored?

Everything — your platform tokens, post history, calendar entries, and theme preference — lives in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any Fed-Poster server because there isn't one. When you post, your browser talks directly to each platform's API. You can export or wipe all data anytime from the Settings page.

What does Pro's "cross-device sync" actually sync?

Pro sync encrypts your credentials, post history, and calendar entries with a passphrase only you know, then stores the encrypted blob in a lightweight relay. Your other devices decrypt it locally. The relay cannot read your data — it only stores opaque ciphertext. This is opt-in and never automatic.

Can I self-host the Pro features too?

Yes. The Self-Host tier gives you the full source, including Pro-equivalent features like unlimited history and queue scheduling. The only Pro-exclusive features are the hosted sync relay and the AI best-time suggestions, which require server-side infrastructure. Everything else is yours to run.

Which platforms are supported?

Currently: Telegram (Bot API), Bluesky (AT Protocol), Mastodon (any instance via REST API), Discord (webhooks), GitHub Gist (API), Stoat (Supabase storage), and DeviantArt (OAuth, in progress). Multi-post lets you broadcast the same message to several at once with per-platform character limits.

How is this "anti-SaaS"?

Traditional SaaS platforms host your data, control your access, and can shut down or change pricing at any time. Fed-Poster inverts that: the app lives in your browser, your data lives in your browser, and you can fork and self-host the entire thing. We're not a platform you depend on — we're a tool you own.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Absolutely. Pro is month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime and you keep all your data — it just reverts to browser-only storage. Your posts, history, and credentials remain intact in localStorage.
